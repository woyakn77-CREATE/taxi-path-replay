    FAA diagrams print latitude and longitude ticks on the border, and this page reads them to align the chart. If it cannot, click Add control point, click a spot on the diagram such as a border tick or a runway end, and type its coordinates. Two points align a north-up chart. Three or more also correct skew and show the fit error.

    ATC steps

    Left out of the steps (0)

      How the steps are drawn. Each ATC call for this aircraft starts a step. The colored line is the ADS-B track from that call until the next one, so it shows what the aircraft did after each instruction. Hold points are stops of 15 seconds or more after a hold-short instruction. Readback checks compare taxiways, runways, hold-short points, frequencies, speeds, gates and spots between the instruction and the reply.

      Limits. Transcripts are machine-made and miss words, so a flagged readback may be a transcription gap. Chart labels near the path are a rough cue, not a route check. Not for navigation.
